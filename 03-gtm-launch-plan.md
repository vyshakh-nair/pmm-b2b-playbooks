The cross-functional sequence from "ready to ship" to "market knows it exists."

## 1. Launch Overview

- **Release name:** *[Product / Feature Name]*
- **Tier:** *[1: flagship / 2: feature expansion / 3: minor patch]*
- **Target date:** *[YYYY-MM-DD]*
- **Primary KPI:** *[e.g., 50 enterprise-qualified pipeline leads within 60 days]*

## 2. Checklist

### Pre-launch — product readiness & positioning
- [ ] Messaging matrix and positioning locked *(PMM)*
- [ ] Sales reps trained on the pitch, not just the deck *(PMM + Product)*
- [ ] Case studies or early testimonials in hand *(PMM)*
- [ ] Pricing and contract terms finalized *(RevOps)*

### Launch day — market announcement
- [ ] Blog post and exec announcement live *(Content/PMM)*
- [ ] Paid and organic social running *(Demand Gen)*
- [ ] Website and docs updated *(PMM/Web)*
- [ ] Email out to the existing customer base *(Marketing Ops)*

### Post-launch — adoption & measurement
- [ ] Feature adoption tracked *(Product Analytics)*
- [ ] Win rates vs. named competitors tracked *(RevOps/PMM)*
- [ ] Customer feedback logged for the next iteration *(Product/PMM)*

**Note:** the pre-launch phase is where most delays happen — pricing sign-off and sales readiness are the usual bottlenecks, so start those earliest.
