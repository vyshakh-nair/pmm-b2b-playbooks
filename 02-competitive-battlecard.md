# Competitive Battlecard

For reps to use before or during a deal against a named competitor.

**Product:** *[Your Product]* **vs.** *[Competitor Name]*

## 1. Quick Glance

- **We win when:** *[complex multi-cloud, automated GTM workflows, lower TCO]*
- **They win when:** *[legacy on-prem, long-term enterprise contracts already locked in]*
- **Our differentiator:** *[native GenAI automation vs. their manual rules engine]*

## 2. Where They're Weak

**Operational complexity**
Ask the prospect how many engineering hours they burn each quarter maintaining custom integration scripts. Most reps get an honest, and uncomfortable, answer.
Our angle: native integrations, no scripts to maintain > Translate to benefits > e.g. time saved, token allocations for AI tools redirected to other priorities etc.

**Pricing that moves on them**
Ask if their current vendor charges overage fees when data volume spikes.
Our angle: pricing is capped, so a bad month doesn't blow the budget.

## 3. Objections You'll Actually Hear

**"Competitor X has been around longer, it's the safe pick."**
Fair — they built the category. But that architecture needs constant manual oversight. We get you to value faster and cut daily ops work roughly in half. Ask what "safe" is actually costing them in headcount.
