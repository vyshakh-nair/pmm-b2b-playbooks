# Objection-Handling Battlecard

For late-stage deals where the prospect is stalling, comparing, or pushing on price. Treat the scripts as starting points. Read one verbatim and the prospect will hear a script.

**Product:** *[Your Product]*

## 1. The Objections That Actually Kill Deals

List these in the order you hear them, not alphabetically. Frequency tells you where to spend prep time.

**"This is too expensive compared to [Competitor]."**
Usually a sticker-price comparison, not a TCO one. Ask what the current workaround costs them today - hours per week, headcount, whatever it is. Get a real number before you talk about yours. Don't just match the discount. You'll win the deal but lose the account.

**"We already have budget approved for [Competitor]."**
The competitor's champion sold this internally already. Find out who owns the budget line and ask directly. Don't argue against the decision that's already been made. Sell them a cause to revisit.

**"We need to check with legal, security, or procurement first."**
Sometimes real, sometimes a polite stall. Offer to send your security docs to their team now, so you're not the bottleneck sitting in someone's inbox. If they say "I'll circle back," get them to commit to a timeline before you hang up.

**"Can we revisit this next quarter?"**
Priorities shifted, or you lost the champion somewhere along the way. Ask what changed since the last call. If it's a budget cycle, get a specific date on the calendar — not "sometime in Q2." Don't quietly push it in your own pipeline without confirming the urgency is still real.

## 2. Objection → Proof Point Map

| Objection | Proof point to lead with |
|---|---|
| Price | *[ROI calculator result, payback period]* |
| Risk or trust | *[Customer reference, security certification]* |
| Feature gap | *[Roadmap commitment, workaround shipping this quarter]* |
| Timing | *[Cost of waiting, competitor's pricing changes]* |

If the same objection turns up in three deals in a row, it's not a sales problem anymore. Flag it to Product or Pricing.
