# Product Positioning & Messaging Framework

Use this to get Product, Sales, and Marketing saying the same thing about a product or major feature. 

Remember: People sell to people. The pain points don't stop at the business' but extends to individual pain points of the buyer too.

## 1. Target Audience & Personas

**Primary Buyer Persona:** *[e.g., CISO / VP of Security]*
- **Pain points:** *[alert fatigue, high MTTR, compliance risk, fragmented tool stack, political pressure, justify recent promotion]*
- **What they're actually trying to do:** *[cut risk, consolidate vendors, run a leaner team, Extend their influence in the organization, Handle more responsibility]*

**Primary User Persona:** *[e.g., SOC Tier-1 Analyst]*
- **Pain points:** *[manual triage, switching between five tools, chasing false positives]*
- **Day-to-day workflow:** *[detection, log analysis, mitigation]*

## 2. Positioning Statement

> For **[target audience]** who **[need or opportunity]**, **[product name]** is a **[category]** that **[benefit]**. Unlike **[main alternative]**, we **[differentiator]**.

## 3. Value Pillars & Messaging Matrix

| Pillar | Key Benefit | Underlying Feature | Proof point |
|---|---|---|---|
| Velocity | Cuts incident response time in half | GenAI threat summaries | MTTR drops from 4 hours to 45 minutes |
| Cost | Lower ingestion spend | Telemetry routing & filtering | 30% cut in SIEM ingestion fees |
| Adoption | Live in minutes, no agents to install | API-first cloud connector | Setup done in under 15 minutes |

**Notes**
- Fill the matrix with real data where you have them.
- If two pillars end up saying the same thing in different words, cut one.
