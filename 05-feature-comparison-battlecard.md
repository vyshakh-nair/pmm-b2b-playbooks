# Feature Comparison Battlecard

For deals where the prospect is running a checklist against a named competitor. Keep this honest. A comparison that hides your gaps gets caught in the demo, and the rest of the deal pays for it.

**Product:** *[Your Product]* **vs.** *[Competitor Name]*

## 1. Feature-by-Feature

| Capability | Us | [Competitor] | Why it matters to them |
|---|---|---|---|
| *[e.g., Real-time alerting]* | ✅ Native | ⚠️ Add-on, extra cost | *[Faster time to detection]* |
| *[e.g., Multi-cloud support]* | ✅ AWS, Azure, GCP | ✅ AWS, Azure only | *[Only relevant if they run GCP]* |
| *[e.g., Custom dashboards]* | ⚠️ Limited templates today | ✅ Full customization | *[Be straight about this one]* |
| *[e.g., API access]* | ✅ Full REST API | ✅ Full REST API | *[Parity — don't spend time here]* |

Use the symbols, not paragraphs. If a rep needs three sentences to explain a row, the row is wrong.

## 2. Pricing Shape

| | Us | [Competitor] |
|---|---|---|
| Entry tier | *[$X/mo, what's included]* | *[their entry price, what's excluded]* |
| Usage-based costs | *[capped or uncapped, overage terms]* | *[same]* |
| Contract minimum | *[annual only, or monthly available]* | *[same]* |

## 3. Roadmap — What to Say and What Not To

**Committed and dated:** *[feature, benefit, target quarter]*. Safe to mention, with the date attached.

**In discovery, no date:** *[feature/benefit]*. Don't promise a quarter. Say it's being explored.

**Not on the roadmap:** if they ask directly, say so. A prospect who catches you overselling the roadmap stops trusting the rest of the pitch too. Communicate what you'll provide. Remember, your team are your first customers, so overselling and leading to scope creep kills NPS scores.

## 4. Where We Genuinely Lose to [Competitor Name]

Two or three real gaps. Not softened ones. If this section is empty, nobody's reviewed it since launch. Find someone who lost a deal to this competitor and ask why.
